# Liberation Movements & Allied Organizations

## 🚩 Historical Revolutionary Movements

### ✊🏽 La Raza Unida Party (1970)

**Partido Nacional de La Raza Unida**

Founded in Texas to challenge two-party system and fight for Chicano self-determination.

**Key Figures:**
- José Ángel Gutiérrez (Crystal City, TX)
- Rodolfo "Corky" Gonzales (Denver, CO)
- Reies López Tijerina (Land Grant Movement)

**Core Demands:**
- Educational reform (bilingual education, Chicano curriculum)
- Land rights restoration (Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo)
- Political representation
- Economic justice for farmworkers and laborers
- Cultural autonomy (Aztlán concept)

**Tactics:**
- Third-party electoral campaigns
- School walkouts and boycotts
- Community organizing
- Cultural resistance and education
- Coalition with other POC movements

**Legacy:** Paved way for modern Chicano/Latino political power

---

### 🐻 Brown Berets (1967-Present)

**Chicano Youth Liberation Organization**

Formed in Los Angeles, inspired by Black Panthers, focused on Chicano community defense.

**Key Figures:**
- David Sánchez (founder)
- Carlos Montes (organizer, still active)
- Gloria Arellanes (women's leadership)

**Community Programs:**
- Free breakfast programs
- Health clinics
- Community patrols (police accountability)
- Educational workshops
- Anti-war organizing (Vietnam)

**Tactics:**
- Armed self-defense (legal open carry era)
- Direct action protests
- Mutual aid networks
- Coalition building (with Panthers, Young Lords)
- Cultural education (Chicano identity)

**Modern Activity:** Reformed 1990s, active in immigration rights, police accountability

### 🐾 Black Panther Party for Self-Defense (1966-1982)

**Revolutionary Black Nationalist & Socialist Organization**

Founded in Oakland to combat police brutality, evolved into revolutionary socialist party.

**Founders & Key Leaders:**
- Huey P. Newton (Minister of Defense, theorist)
- Bobby Seale (Chairman)
- Eldridge Cleaver (Minister of Information)
- Fred Hampton (Chicago, assassinated by FBI/police 1969)
- Kathleen Cleaver (Communications Secretary)
- Angela Davis (philosopher, organizer)
- Assata Shakur (liberation fighter, exiled to Cuba)

#### 📋 10-Point Program

1. **Freedom** - Self-determination for Black communities
2. **Employment** - Full employment or guaranteed income
3. **End Robbery** - Stop capitalist exploitation of Black community
4. **Housing** - Decent housing fit for human beings
5. **Education** - Education that teaches true history
6. **Healthcare** - Free healthcare for all
7. **End Police Brutality** - Immediate end to police murder
8. **End Wars** - End all wars of aggression (Vietnam)
9. **Freedom for Prisoners** - Free all political prisoners
10. **Land, Bread, Housing, Education, Clothing, Justice, Peace**

#### 🍳 Survival Programs ("Serve the People")

**Community Self-Help Programs:**
- **Free Breakfast for Children** (served 10,000+ kids daily at peak)
- **People's Free Medical Clinics** (13 clinics nationwide)
- **Liberation Schools** (alternative education)
- **Free Clothing Program**
- **Sickle Cell Anemia Testing** (pioneered awareness)
- **Free Ambulance Service**
- **Free Pest Control**
- **Legal Aid & Court Monitoring**
- **Elderly Care Programs**

These programs demonstrated socialism in action and built community power.

#### ⚡ Revolutionary Tactics

**Community Defense:**
- Legal armed patrols (California open carry, pre-Mulford Act)
- Copwatching with law books and cameras
- Community accountability for police

**Political Education:**
- Required reading: Fanon, Mao, Marx, Lenin
- Political education classes
- Study groups on revolutionary theory

**Coalition Building:**
- Rainbow Coalition (Panthers, Young Lords, Young Patriots)
- International solidarity (Cuba, Algeria, Vietnam, Palestine)
- Cross-racial organizing with poor whites

**Media & Propaganda:**
- *The Black Panther* newspaper (circulation 250,000)
- Revolutionary art and graphics
- Public speaking tours

**Key Concepts:**
- **Revolutionary Intercommunalism** (Huey Newton's theory)
- **Serve the People** (mutual aid as revolution)
- **All Power to the People**
- **Self-Defense is Self-Respect**

#### 🎯 FBI Repression (COINTELPRO)

Panthers were #1 target of FBI counterintelligence:
- Assassination of Fred Hampton (1969)
- Infiltration and disruption
- False flag operations
- Mass incarceration of leadership
- Media demonization

Despite repression, Panthers demonstrated revolutionary organizing works.

## 🤝 Modern Allied Organizations (2026)

### 🌹 Socialist & Communist

- **PSL** (Party for Socialism and Liberation) - Revolutionary Marxist
- **DSA** (Democratic Socialists of America) - Electoral + organizing
- **FRSO** (Freedom Road Socialist Organization) - Marxist-Leninist
- **CPUSA** (Communist Party USA) - Broad left coalition

### ✊🏿 Black Liberation

- **Black Lives Matter** (decentralized network)
- **Movement for Black Lives (M4BL)** - Coalition with policy platform
- **Black Youth Project 100 (BYP100)** - Abolitionist organizing
- **Dream Defenders** - Florida-based, prison abolition
- **Malcolm X Grassroots Movement** - Revolutionary nationalism
- **Black Alliance for Peace** - Anti-imperialist

**Demands:**
- Defund/abolish police and prisons
- Reparations for slavery and Jim Crow
- Community control of institutions
- Economic justice and redistribution
- Political power and self-determination

### ✊🏽 Chicano/Latino Liberation

- **Mijente** - Digital + on-ground Latinx organizing
- **United We Dream** - Undocumented youth movement
- **NDLON** (National Day Laborer Organizing Network)
- **Cosecha Movement** - Noncooperation tactics for immigrant rights
- **Brown Berets** - Continued community defense work

### 🪶 Indigenous Sovereignty

- **Indigenous Environmental Network** - Climate + land defense
- **NDN Collective** - Indigenous liberation and solidarity
- **Red Nation** - Indigenous Marxist organization
- **Land Back Movement** - Restoration of stolen lands
