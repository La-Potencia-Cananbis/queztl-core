# PSL Integration & Networking

## Party for Socialism and Liberation

### 🚩 Overview
Revolutionary Marxist party in the United States focused on building a movement for socialist revolution and international solidarity.

### 📚 Key Educational Resources

**Liberation School** (liberationschool.org)
- Marxist economics & theory
- Anti-imperialism & international solidarity
- Black liberation & racial justice
- Women's & LGBTQ+ liberation
- Labor organizing & workers' power
- Revolutionary strategy & tactics

**Breaking the Chains Magazine**
- Analysis of current struggles
- Revolutionary perspective on world events
- Workers' rights coverage
- Anti-war movement updates

1. **Anti-War Movement**

   - Solidarity with global resistance

2. **Homes Guarantee**
   - Housing as a human right
   - End homelessness & gentrification

3. **Palestinian Solidarity**
   - End occupation & apartheid
   - Self-determination

4. **Cuba Solidarity**
   - End U.S. blockade
   - Defend socialist construction

5. **Workers' Rights**
   - Union organizing support
   - Fight for living wages

6. **Police Abolition**

   - End state violence
